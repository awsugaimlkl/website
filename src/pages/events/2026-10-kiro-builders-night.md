## About the evening

Agent harnesses are the tools and scaffolding that turn a raw language model into an agent that gets work done. Kiro, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenClaw and Windsurf are all examples. The talks look at how these harnesses work, what they do well and where they fall short, and the evening includes a live harness demo and time to compare notes with other AI builders in Kuala Lumpur.

It is for anyone building with AI: engineers, founders and tinkerers, whether you use a harness every day or are just curious.
