# Volunteer photos

Put one photo per volunteer in this folder, named to match the `photo` value in the event's `.md` file. For example, `photo: "jane-tan"` uses `jane-tan.png`.

For the look on the Community Day page, use a PNG with the background removed: the person then stands in front of the purple V, like a cut-out. Crop it close at the sides and just above the head; the tile shows the top of the photo, from the head down to the chest, and cuts off the rest. About 600 pixels wide is plenty. The site turns it black and white.

A JPG (or a PNG that still has its background) also works. It fills the whole tile in black and white and covers the V.

A volunteer without a photo is shown with their initials.
